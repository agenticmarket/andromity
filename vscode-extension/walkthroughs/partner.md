### Andromity helps you write, plan, and understand code right in VS Code.

Andromity can read your files, make edits, run terminal commands, and help you navigate complex codebases. It understands context and works alongside you like an autonomous coding partner.

Ready to get started? Open the chat panel anytime or use the shortcut **Ctrl+Alt+C** (**Cmd+Alt+C** on macOS).
