### Total governance: configure workspace trust and safety modes.

Manage folder permissions and decide how much autonomy to grant Andromity. Choose between Safe mode for explicit review on every action, Trust mode for project workspace edits, or Full and YOLO modes for autonomous execution.

Grant trust to your project directories or adjust your permission mode anytime in the settings panel.
